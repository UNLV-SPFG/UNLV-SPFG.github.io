This week, [Madeline Overton](/team/overton-madeline/) and [Peter Quigley](/team/quigley-peter/) presented at [LIAC42 - To Be or not to Be : Understanding the star, the disk, and the stripped companions](https://www.gaphe.uliege.be/cms/c_13589217/en/gaphe-liac42), held by the IAU working group on active B-stars and hosted by the University of Liège. The workshop was organized around four main themes: 
- Distinguishing characteristics of Be stars
- The [companions](/research/protoplanetary-disks/#binary-companions) to Be stars
- Physics of the outflowing gaseous [disks](/research/)
- Single and multiple-star evolutionary channels
